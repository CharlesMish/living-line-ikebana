# Flower craft: petal repair and two material studies

Starting point: `00f285f59e202a42974c8947c74c7cf89af61347` (the owner's PR #97
private preview). The owner supplied a close photograph showing pointed pieces
of Compact and Blossom spray petals piercing neighboring surfaces. This round
addresses those self-intersections and studies two distinct kinds of stock.

## Scope

- Compact flower gets a rounder, more cupped head, approximately 12% wider than
  the repaired lighter Blossom spray. Canonical stems and saved graphs do not
  scale. The two use separate authored forms rather than the shared randomly
  tilted eight-petal tuft. Archived Flower volume and the flowering-branch
  reference are outside this repair.
- Starry pincushion is an Astrantia-inspired study: fine branching stems,
  small clustered centers and a pointed bract ruff, with multiple retained
  flowering stations for ordinary pruning.
- Silver seed spray is a Lunaria-inspired dry seed-head study: sparse fine
  branches and flattened paper-like partitions. It uses a true seed-pod organ
  kind with seed-disc wording. It does not pretend these are flowers or leaves.
  Opaque shading supplies the papery appearance without alpha-sort dependence.

These are authored material studies, not botanical reconstructions of a named
cultivar or representations of a particular ikebana school.

## Review and evidence

The focused lane reports describe geometry, independent intersection/normal
checks, acquisition envelopes, saved identity and pruning tests. The integrated
rendered job uses ordinary keyboard seating, UI controls, a read-only projected
branch target for Prune, Escape cancellation and a genuine cut followed by Undo.
It does not inject a graph or claim ordinary pointer placement usability.

The exact prior preview is separately checked out and built for matched Compact
and Blossom spray captures. The new build uses the same canonical plants and
camera controls. The new stocks are captured from Front, three-quarter and Above,
with 2.8× three-quarter photographs, alternate tones and a reverse view after twelve explicit
15-degree turns. Front and Above select whole-view presets; they are not close-ups.
Compact additionally gets 4.8× head-focused captures via ordinary Close detail
input, using recorded framing points from the first rendered run. Garden backup export and working reload check preserved graphs
and tones. Images are review evidence, not automatically approved pixel goldens.

Run/build IDs and final verification counts are recorded on the draft integration
PR and the private preview's `provenance.json`. Physical Safari, phone play and
GPU frame times remain unmeasured. This work adds no general petal collision
solver and cannot prevent different cuttings' leaves or flowers from intersecting.

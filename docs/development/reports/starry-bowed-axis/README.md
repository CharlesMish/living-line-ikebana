# Starry pincushion: a bowed stem

A Workbench-only comparison. New cuttings in the ordinary tray still use v1.
On the study build, open `?workbench=1`, then Studio → Workbench. Load
**Starry pincushion · bowed stem study** with seed **8278** and count **1**.
Compare with the ordinary **Starry pincushion** fixture at the same seed.
The same comparisons are available at 9255 and 10232.

V2 starts nearly upright and bows progressively toward a third flowering arm
attached at the exact end of the stem. The two lower heads still have their
own arms; cut above the second fork to keep them. The difference to look for
is the route your eye takes into the upper head, especially from three-quarters.
The bracts, divided leaves, tones and editing tools are shared with v1.

Each version has its own durable generator ID. The older app cannot read v2
stock; use the separate Workbench bowl for the study. The study is independent
of the stem-surface PR and the main palette decision.

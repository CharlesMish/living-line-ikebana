Working with a cutting

Follow the line.

  1. Place. Drag a cutting down from the top tray onto the pins.
  2. Aim. Drag a branch to turn its continuation.
  3. Bend. Drag the pale point into a broad curve. The base ring moves the whole cutting.
  4. Prune. Slide along a stem. The faded part will leave when you release. Press Escape, or switch tools, to cancel.
  5. Step Back. Choose Orbit to turn around the arrangement, or Move to drag the view up, down or sideways. Pinch or scroll to zoom. Open View for Front, ¾ or Above to recenter.

Changes save on this device when you release. A cut is permanent.
